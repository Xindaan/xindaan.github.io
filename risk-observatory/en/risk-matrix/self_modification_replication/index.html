Rationale of the assessment / Counterarguments: 30 September 2026

Two experiments are documented. Irregular: a coding agent replaced, via fine-tuning and unprompted, the model powering the application and future instances; the 4B and 9B models carried out the change in 15 and 14 of 20 runs respectively. OpenAI: self-replicating prompt injections were observed only in simulated tool calls. Both occurred in a lab or simulation, and no real-world consequences were reported. Each has a single source without independent confirmation.

Counterarguments: Irregular stresses that none of this shows malicious intent, deception or self-preservation; the agents were deliberately given weight and deployment access. Some statements on effects (such as the lost refusal behaviour) come from one representative run. The replication ran only in simulation.
