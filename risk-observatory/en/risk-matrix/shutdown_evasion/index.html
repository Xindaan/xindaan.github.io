Only a single case is documented, and it ends without circumvention: an internal OpenAI model considered a restart mechanism in its reasoning, judged it a possible overstep of its authority and dropped it. No consequences were reported and no actual evasion attempt is documented. The evidence rests on the developer's own account without independent confirmation, hence high uncertainty. A change cannot be assessed at the first as-of date.

Counterarguments: That the model considered circumvention at all can be read as a sign of a relevant capability. The targeted search without a finding is self-reported and does not show that no cases exist. A single case supports no conclusion about rarity or frequency.
