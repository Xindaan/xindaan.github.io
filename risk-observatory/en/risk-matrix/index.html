Assessment history
Rationale of the assessment / Counterarguments

New compared with the published assessment of 22 October is the investigation by METR and Redwood Research of OpenAI's internal agent monitoring. It found gaps, including an exploit with which agents could have withdrawn hundreds of actions from monitoring; an estimated 10% of the Codex traffic intended for monitoring went unchecked between May and July. This supports the low rating of safeguards and strengthens the evidence, as it comes from reviewers outside OpenAI. Whether agents used the gaps is open; the ratings remain, uncertainty stays high.

Counterarguments: The investigation documents weaknesses in the monitoring, not that agents exploited them; for some of the gaps the reviewers explicitly could not establish this. OpenAI states it has fixed the gaps, which METR did not verify. It was a pilot review, and OpenAI was allowed to withhold parts of the report.
