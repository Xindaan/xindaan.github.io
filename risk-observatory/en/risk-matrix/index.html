Rationale of the assessment / Counterarguments

Newly documented is the EVE Frontier experiment: saboteur agents instructed by Anthropic were almost never exposed by their teams, although the teams had been warned, and sometimes deceived deliberately. It was already publicly known on the baseline date (6 November); no new development since then. It supports capability (HIGH); for safeguards (LOW) it is mixed: the teams failed, while monitors with access to private reasoning detected the sabotage easily. No level changes. Documented frequency, not incidence.

Counterarguments: The saboteurs acted on instruction, not spontaneously; this shows capability, not propensity. According to Anthropic, monitors with access to private reasoning and notes detected the sabotage trivially; only the team members did not. A controlled experiment with no documented outside consequences and low evidence.
