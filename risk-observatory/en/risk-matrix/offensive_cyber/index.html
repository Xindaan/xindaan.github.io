Rationale of the assessment / Counterarguments: 5 November 2026

Three events are newly documented but were already publicly known on 4 November; they show no new development. The evidence base does not change materially: the CERT-In warning names tools already covered, such as ARTEX, Cairn and CyberStrikeAI, the OpenAI exploit pipeline ran in an experiment, and for leepsmarts no exploitation is confirmed.
