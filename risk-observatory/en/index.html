Positive developments in safety measures

  • ImplementedGoogle DeepMind: Gemini 4 Carbon first for cyber defenders in the Fairwind Program

    According to Google DeepMind, a group of cyber defenders received Gemini 4 Carbon through the Fairwind Program two weeks before the model was generally released. People working in cyber defence can apply to the programme on a rolling basis and gain access to Carbon for cyber through it. In simulated cyberattack test environments, Carbon's safeguards cut the per-attempt breach rate to 0.8%, compared with 3.1% for Argon. Self-reported.

    According to METR, it shares Anthropic's view that the models as of 15 July posed only low catastrophic risks. METR nevertheless considers it plausible that these models had already crossed Anthropic's early warning threshold at that point. The Risk Report's contrary finding rests, METR says, on debatable judgement calls that the public report does not disclose clearly.

  • ImplementedAnthropic's IPO filing says its own threshold for AI-accelerated development may be near

    According to its amended IPO prospectus (S-1/A), internal estimates and expectations for upcoming models suggest Anthropic may soon reach its Responsible Scaling Policy threshold for “dramatically accelerated progress attributable to AI”; no current model is designated above it. Strong acceleration has so far been noticed only in hindsight. Anthropic does not expect Claude to safely build future frontier models autonomously without meaningful human oversight; policy changes are under review.
