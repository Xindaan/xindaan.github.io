1c:T47e,Language models may assist with extraction, classification and summarisation. An AI-generated summary is, however, never evidence in itself: it can only be recorded as the provenance of a claim. A claim counts as supported only once it has been checked against the original source and the location is recorded with a verbatim quote. This check is performed by AI models: one model reads the archived source text and proposes location, quote and status; a second, independent model cross-checks. A script ensures every quote appears verbatim in the source. If the two models disagree, a third model judges independently; only what two models agree on counts, otherwise the claim is not supported. The verification note records which models checked. Models likewise assess each event (evidence quality, event type, attribution, texts): one model proposes, a second rates blind and checks every text against the supported claims, a third resolves differences. The system enforces these rules on every build; publication without a check is not possible.
