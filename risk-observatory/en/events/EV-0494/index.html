123 Reg and GoDaddy report “AI-driven” attack with customer data exposure

According to The Register and Credit Union Daily, 123 Reg and GoDaddy described an attack of 18 September 2026 as “AI-driven”. Customer contact and product data were obtained via an API.

Observed behaviour
According to The Register, 123 Reg called the 18 September intrusion “the first known AI-driven cyberattack” against the company and sees early signs pointing to a China-nexus attacker. A spokesperson said the investigation identified AI cyberattack “skills” hosted on Chinese infrastructure, including copies of skills open sourced by Anthropic and several other AI exploitation frameworks and scripts. According to Credit Union Daily, GoDaddy reported an AI-driven attack on the same date; a third party obtained profile and setting information of users of the Websites + Marketing product via an API. GoDaddy detected the incident on 19 September.
Demonstrated consequences
According to 123 Reg, contact information, “related customer relationship data”, VAT numbers and product information from an API were compromised. According to GoDaddy, the third party obtained profile and setting information, including contact information supplied for publication on the website.
Severity
Moderate
Attribution
Both companies describe the attack as AI-driven; 123 Reg sees early signs of a China-nexus attacker. How exactly AI was used is not documented in the sources, and the attribution is unconfirmed. (Attribution confidence: Medium)
Independent confirmation
None
Rationale of the assessment
Both accounts come from the affected companies and are relayed by media (The Register, Credit Union Daily); they are self-reports without independent technical verification. The statements on the exposed data are documented, the AI share is only asserted.
