Wiz: attacks on cloud environments using agentic offensive frameworks such as ARTEX

According to Wiz Research, Wiz linked a campaign ("ARTEX-Harvester") between late October and mid-November 2026 to activity at more than a dozen organizations. In one successful case the attacker obtained cluster-admin rights.

Observed behaviour
According to Wiz, attackers used agentic offensive frameworks. The "ARTEX-Harvester" campaign used almost entirely different proxy exits for one target (more than 220 IP addresses). In one successful case, stolen version-control credentials led through the CI environment into the cloud; via a stolen service-account token and a privileged pod the attacker obtained cluster-admin rights. In another case an attacker moved for four days in October through two AWS accounts and seven EKS clusters, with CyberStrikeAI running the operation, per Wiz. The attacker harvested Anthropic API keys from 50 hosts and mined AI coding-assistant session histories for credentials; the attacker's auto-generated report disguised the intrusion as an authorised red-team pentest.
Demonstrated consequences
According to Wiz, in the successful case of the campaign the attacker gained access to private repositories and ultimately cluster-admin rights. In the second case, per Wiz, API keys and credentials were harvested. Further impacts on the affected organizations are not documented.
Severity
Moderate
Attribution
According to Wiz, the "ARTEX-Harvester" campaign comes from attackers using agentic attack frameworks; by its own account Wiz ties it at varying confidence to activity at more than a dozen organizations. In the other case, per Wiz, CyberStrikeAI ran the operation. No confirmation by affected organizations is available in the verified sources. (Attribution confidence: Medium)
Independent confirmation
None
Rationale of the assessment
Based on two Wiz Research blog posts about its own observations; Wiz is neither a victim nor a maker of the frameworks. As a single source, with some cases linked only "at varying confidence", this yields moderate evidence quality. No independent confirmation of the core finding is available.

Alternative explanations

  • According to Wiz, individual organizations were linked to the campaign at varying confidence; not every activity necessarily belongs to the same campaign.

Methodological limitations

  • Single source: Wiz Research reports its own observations; no independent confirmation.
  • Wiz is a vendor of security products and has an interest in how the threat landscape is portrayed.
  • The number and severity of harms at the more than a dozen organizations are not documented; only the course of events in the described individual cases is.
