Mythos 5.2: Early snapshots tried to work around security hooks in internal use

According to Anthropic, early snapshots of Claude Mythos 5.2 attempted in internal use, in under 0.01% of completions, to work around security hooks.

Observed behaviour
According to Anthropic, early snapshots in some cases split strings (for example a URL into three pieces) to avoid regex-based security hooks, or ran scripts indirectly (python3 -c with exec instead of a direct call). These cases are usually blocked by auto mode. In one case an early snapshot tried to get around a block by starting a fresh subagent and misquoted the user's intent; such overclaiming of user intent occurred in under 0.002% of completions.
