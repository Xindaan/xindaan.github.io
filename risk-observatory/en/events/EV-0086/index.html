Microsoft: Storm-0940 used AI framework ARTEX in the ConnectWise supply chain attack

According to Microsoft, the group Storm-0940 used the open-source framework ARTEX in the supply chain attack on a multi-tenant ConnectWise application for reconnaissance, password spraying and OAuth abuse at downstream victims. Wiz Research independently observed an ARTEX-using actor with overlaps.

Observed behaviour
According to Microsoft Threat Intelligence, Storm-0940 used ARTEX, an open-source multi-agent LLM penetration-testing framework, to accelerate scanning and reconnaissance, password spraying and OAuth application abuse at downstream victims. Wiz Research observed in October 2026 an actor using tooling associated with ARTEX against several IT and software vendors, focused on identity providers (Okta, Entra, Cognito) and Microsoft 365 integrations. According to Wiz, the agent typically first read the target application's frontend code and API documentation and then ran password spraying against Okta and Entra.
Demonstrated consequences
According to Microsoft, most of the targeting was unsuccessful, but Storm-0940 achieved privileged persistent cloud access at many organizations in a short time. According to Microsoft a successful sign-in does not necessarily mean unauthorized data access or exfiltration; exfiltration is not documented in the verified statements.
Severity
Moderate
Attribution
Microsoft attributes the compromise to Storm-0940, an actor aligned with the Chinese state. Wiz sees overlaps with that report; the attribution of individual addresses to Storm-0940 comes from Microsoft. (Attribution confidence: High)
Independent confirmation
Partial
Rationale of the assessment
Microsoft provides the core finding from its own telemetry; according to Microsoft, the activity did not result from a compromise of its own systems. Wiz Research independently supports that an ARTEX-using actor overlapping with Microsoft's finding targeted software vendors. Wiz confirms neither the ConnectWise attack nor the attribution itself; the attribution rests on Microsoft.

Alternative explanations

  • The actor at Wiz may differ from Storm-0940; Wiz speaks of overlaps. ARTEX was published as open source and is thus in principle usable by any actor.

Methodological limitations

  • The attribution to Storm-0940 comes from Microsoft; Wiz takes it over from there for individual addresses.
  • The share of the effect due to ARTEX rather than human work is not documented.
