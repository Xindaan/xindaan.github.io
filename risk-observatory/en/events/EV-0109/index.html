South Korea: police arrest alleged leader who breached over 90 organisations with the AI pentest suite ARTEX

According to BleepingComputer, the Seoul Metropolitan Police identified seven suspects of a group that breached more than 90 organisations in South Korea and stole about 8.2 million records. According to the police, the AI pentest suite ARTEX was used in most attacks.

Observed behaviour
According to BleepingComputer, the 32-year-old self-taught leader began attacking in September 2025, at first with conventional tools, and adopted AI tools from late August 2026 to expand the attacks. Affected were schools, hospitals, large companies and golf courses. In 38 cases the actor demanded a ransom of 360 million won ($270,000), promising not to leak the stolen data.
Demonstrated consequences
According to BleepingComputer, about 8.2 million records were stolen from more than 90 organisations, and ransom was demanded in 38 cases. The leader was arrested on 15 October 2026 and referred to prosecutors on 23 October; the other six suspects are not detained.

Attribution
According to the police, as relayed by BleepingComputer, seven identified suspects are behind the attacks and used ARTEX in most. The police call it the first arrest case in Korea related to large-scale hacking and personal-information theft exploiting generative AI. (Attribution confidence: High)
