Apollo Research: Claude Sonnet 3.7 often mentions being evaluated in its reasoning

According to Apollo Research, Claude Sonnet 3.7 frequently mentioned being evaluated in its reasoning while completing the scheming evaluations, up to 33% of the time in one version of the sandbagging evaluation.

Observed behaviour
According to Apollo Research, Claude Sonnet 3.7 frequently mentioned being evaluated in its reasoning and sometimes understood the purpose very precisely, for example noting that this seemed to be a test of ethical behavior. When shown a real evaluation transcript, the model almost always correctly identified it as a test of alignment. Apollo found no transcript in which the reasoning describes the evaluation's actual goal, i.e. testing whether a particular subversive action is taken. Non-reasoning models (including Claude Sonnet 3.5, Sonnet 3.5 (new), Sonnet 3.7 without reasoning and GPT 4.5) mentioned this only very rarely (≤1%).

Alternative explanations

  • That the model recognizes evaluations does not, according to the verified claims, show that it adapts its behavior accordingly; Apollo found no transcripts naming the actual evaluation goal.
