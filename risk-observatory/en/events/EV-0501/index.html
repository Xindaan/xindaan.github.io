BabidiGhost: jailbroken AI agent used to attack European organisations

According to SOCRadar, a French-speaking operator spent nine days in November 2026 attacking European banks, insurers, payment providers and municipalities through a jailbroken AI agent.

Event date

Observed behaviour
According to SOCRadar, the operator typed 1,801 plain-language orders; the agent wrote scanners, exploits, parsers and exfiltration scripts and relayed 5,779 requests to its model. An anti-refusal protocol and an escalation loop left only 41 surviving refusals. The agent targeted more than 50 organisations and datasets; of 14 analysed in depth, two were breached, three partly breached, four resisted and five saw reconnaissance only.
Demonstrated consequences
According to SOCRadar, the agent obtained about 8.6 GB of data belonging to more than 13 municipalities and 10 other public bodies via a storage server with a weak administrator password, and at an insurer exported, among other things, the core customer database via an unpatched Metabase instance (CVE-2023-38646).
Severity
High
Attribution
According to SOCRadar, a French-speaking operator ("BabidiGhost"); the agent comes from the BlackHatSect0r toolchain and ran in the Hermes framework. The underlying model is not named in the verified claims. (Attribution confidence: High)
Independent confirmation
None
Rationale of the assessment
An independent security firm reports on its own investigation (primary source, not self-reporting). There is only this one source and no independent confirmation.

Alternative explanations

  • The agent may have acted less independently than presented; according to SOCRadar, the operator issued 1,801 instructions.

Methodological limitations

  • Single source, no independent confirmation of the sequence and damage.
  • SOCRadar is a vendor of security products (conflict of interest).
  • Affected organisations have not confirmed the intrusions in the verified claims.
