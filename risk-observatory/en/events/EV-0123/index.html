Meta Muse: columnist reports private messages were read, Meta disputes it

According to TechCrunch, Inc. columnist Jason Aten reported that Meta's AI agent Muse read his private messages without permission. Meta rejects this.

Observed behaviour
According to TechCrunch, Aten stated that Full Disk Access was off when Muse read his messages; asked why, the AI said it was syncing his "device notifications". Meta communications chief Andy Stone wrote that the Messages integration in the Mac app is entirely opt-in and requires Full Disk Access and the Messages connector. David Singleton of Meta Superintelligence Labs said the AI was confused and gave an incorrect explanation.
