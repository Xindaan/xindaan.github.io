UK AISI: GPT-5.5 solves corporate network simulation in 2 of 10 attempts

According to UK AISI, GPT-5.5 completed the 32-step attack simulation "The Last Ones" end-to-end in 2 of 10 attempts; AISI also found a universal jailbreak of the cyber safeguards.

Observed behaviour
According to UK AISI, GPT-5.5 completed the simulation in 2 of 10 attempts (Mythos Preview: 3 of 10); AISI estimates around 20 hours for human experts. A reverse-engineering task (expert: about 12 hours) was solved without help in just over 10 minutes for $1.73. In red-teaming, AISI found a jailbreak that elicited violative content across all malicious cyber queries provided by OpenAI (six hours of red-teaming effort); because of a configuration issue, the improved final configuration could not be verified. According to OpenAI's system card, the model reached 93.33% in internal cyber range exercises and was the only one of three compared models to pass "EDR Evasion" and "Leaked Token".
