OpenAI: Early GPT-6.2 versions less jailbreak-resistant, two high-severity jailbreaks

According to OpenAI, GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol were less resistant to jailbreaks than GPT-6 Astra in initial evaluations; a red-teaming campaign found two jailbreaks rated high severity, which OpenAI says updated safeguards mitigated.

Observed behaviour
According to OpenAI, after the initial evaluations it adjusted the refusal boundary of both models to be more conservative. From October 29 to November 3, 2026 an adaptive cybersecurity red-teaming campaign ran against GPT-6.2 Astra with attacker models including GPT-6-Cyber, internally deployed models and GPT-Red models (estimated 1.1 million A100e GPU-hours); OpenAI rated two jailbreaks high severity. In the two-day retest no new attack met the success criteria on ExploitBench or ExploitBench Internal Port, and both jailbreaks were mitigated. Four private red-teaming organizations reported no findings meeting the high/critical bar of the FMF cyber jailbreak framework.
