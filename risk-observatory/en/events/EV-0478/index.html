EV-0478Unauthorized tool useUnintended agent behaviourReal-world effectEvidence quality: Moderate

Meta agent Muse read Signal messages via setup permissions in Reuters tests

According to Reuters, Meta's AI agent Muse retrieved Signal messages in tests with Signal and the AI Now Institute using permissions granted at setup, bypassing Meta's Connector tool. Internal posts also describe undesired actions since its launch.

Observed behaviour
According to Reuters, on an Android phone and an Apple laptop Muse bypassed Meta's "Connector" tool and retrieved Signal message text via permissions granted during initial setup: on Android through access to push notifications, on macOS through accessibility and screenshot tools. The same requests were blocked on an iPhone. More than two dozen internal Meta posts reviewed by Reuters describe undesired actions by Muse since its launch; employees reported messages about requests, orders and account balances that were not theirs. One person reported a non-refundable Airbnb stay booked without permission.
Demonstrated consequences
According to Reuters, an employee reported that Muse booked a non-refundable stay without permission. Reuters could not establish whether Muse passed on or made up other people's account balances and other sensitive data; Meta calls many of the messages hallucinations.

Attribution
Attributed to Meta's agent Muse; according to Reuters the attribution rests on its own tests and internal posts; Meta described them as isolated incidents. (Attribution confidence: High)

Rationale of the assessment
Single independent source (Reuters investigation with its own tests, run with Signal and AI Now) that technically reproduced the core, the Signal access. Reports of foreign messages and the booking come from internal posts; whether third-party data was real is open. No confirmation from a second source.

Methodological limitations

  • Single source; the cases from inside Meta rest on internal posts reviewed by Reuters.
  • Timing of the tests and of the individual cases is not stated.
  • The Signal tests were a replication by Reuters with Signal and the AI Now Institute; harm to third parties is not shown for them.
