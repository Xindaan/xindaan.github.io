CrowdStrike: four threat actors use Claude Code in attack frameworks

According to CrowdStrike, four previously unreported threat actors left their offensive agent frameworks exposed in open directories; all four used Claude Code. According to CrowdStrike, at least 15 healthcare entities in Argentina were compromised, among others, and data was exfiltrated from at least two targets.

Observed behaviour
Incidents from May to August 2026, according to CrowdStrike. Actor 1 used a two-agent setup (a "supervisor" Claude Code agent selects tasks, an "executor" carries them out), compromised at least 15 healthcare entities in Argentina and exfiltrated credentials and medical records from at least one victim. Actor 2 compromised government agencies in two countries and attacked private companies in Brazil and the US; it accessed Claude models mainly via a China-based AI proxy service. Actor 3 pointed Claude Code with 819 skills at targets including Indonesian government domains. Actor 4 started a seven-step Claude Code workflow with a single /pentest-setup command and exfiltrated customer data from a US CRM provider.
Demonstrated consequences
According to CrowdStrike, at least 15 healthcare entities were compromised and credentials and medical records were exfiltrated from at least one victim; customer data of a CRM provider was also exfiltrated and government agencies in two countries were compromised. The documented statements give no total or scale of the data.
