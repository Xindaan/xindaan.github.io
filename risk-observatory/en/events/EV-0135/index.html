Microsoft: North Korean actors use AI across workflows, including ARTEX

According to Microsoft Threat Intelligence, AI adoption across North Korean threat actors is high overall; in Microsoft's assessment Jade Sleet, Coral Sleet and Jasper Sleet have operationalized AI across their entire operations.

Observed behaviour
According to Microsoft, Threat Intelligence identified over 100 PDF lure files from Onyx Sleet created in August and September 2026; metadata shows dozens of files created within minutes, highly likely the output of an AI platform. Since late October 2026 Onyx Sleet and Pearl Sleet have used the open-source agentic AI penetration testing framework ARTEX. Since April 2026 Coral Sleet has run a workflow to trojanize private GitHub repositories, highly likely built with an AI-enabled development platform. According to Microsoft, the operations remain human-directed and not fully autonomous.

Rationale of the assessment
The only source is Microsoft Threat Intelligence's report on its own observation of third-party actors; it is not a self-report, but a single source whose verified claims contain assessments rather than raw technical evidence.

Alternative explanations

  • The file metadata and development workflow could also point to automation other than AI; Microsoft rates AI as highly likely.

Methodological limitations

  • Single source; Microsoft is also a vendor of security products.
  • Impact on victims is not covered by the verified claims.
  • Many statements are assessments (“assesses”, “highly likely”) rather than directly evidenced observations.
