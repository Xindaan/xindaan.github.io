Kaspersky: AI pentest framework CyberStrikeAI used in attacks on educational institutions

According to Kaspersky, confirmed victims of a campaign included educational institutions in several countries across Africa and Latin America; the CyberStrikeAI framework played a key role after the initial intrusion.

Observed behaviour
According to Kaspersky, in CyberStrikeAI an LLM orchestrator on the command-and-control server plans actions, selects tools and generates commands that an implant executes; the operator defines the task in general terms and the model decides commands and sequence. The agent established persistence and performed local reconnaissance within 2 minutes, followed by domain reconnaissance over 5 hours. The attackers carried out DCSync and Golden Ticket attacks via the agent using a renamed version of mimikatz. The entire attack until compromise of the domain administrators took 3 to 4 days.
Demonstrated consequences
According to Kaspersky, domain administrators were compromised and credentials were harvested (DCSync, Golden Ticket); data exfiltration or outage is not established in the verified statements.
Severity
Moderate
Attribution
Kaspersky attributes the attacks of the campaign to CyberStrikeAI and its LLM orchestrator based on its own technical findings; the underlying model and the attackers are not named in the verified statements. (Attribution confidence: High)
Independent confirmation
None
Rationale of the assessment
Kaspersky reports on its own investigation with technical details and is independent of the victims: a primary source without self-reporting. Single source: MODERATE.
