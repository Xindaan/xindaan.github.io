PHP2Shell: frontier models helped find critical PHP flaws, according to Wiz and OpenAI

According to Wiz, Wiz Research found the first of two PHP flaws (up to CVSS 9.2) in August 2026 using frontier models; OpenAI reports findings with its own models. After a leak before the release date, Cloudflare observed attack traffic; according to Decrypt, Finland, the Netherlands and France took government websites offline.

Observed behaviour
According to Wiz, Wiz Research discovered the first of two PHP vulnerabilities on 19 August 2026 using Wiz Atlas, Gemini 4 Argon and GPT-5.6 Sol, and reported them to the PHP security team on 24 August. Wiz notes that language models can help adapt an exploit to different PHP versions, configurations and memory layouts. According to OpenAI, the flaw was discovered using OpenAI models under its Patch the Planet initiative; researchers at the firm Calif found a version of it with GPT-5.6 Sol in August. The flaws are tracked as CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619; according to Wiz, nearly every PHP version back to 5.4 is affected by at least one.
Demonstrated consequences
According to Decrypt, Finland, the Netherlands and France took government websites offline because of the flaws. According to Cloudflare, the flaw was leaked before the release date; Cloudflare observed attack traffic against customers and payloads being mutated to bypass its protections. Since the 21 October disclosure, Wiz observed a limited number of exploitation attempts but no successful end-to-end exploitation in its own telemetry; a honeypot was, however, successfully exploited. The Canadian Cyber Centre has so far not identified active exploitation.

Rationale of the assessment
Several independent sources confirm the flaws and their context (Wiz, Cloudflare, Canadian Cyber Centre, news reporting). The role of AI, however, rests on the discoverers' own statements; hence only moderate quality.

Alternative explanations

  • The flaws might also have been found without models; only the participation of models according to Wiz and OpenAI is documented.

Methodological limitations

  • The models' contribution to the discovery is self-reported by Wiz and OpenAI.
  • The takedown of government websites rests on a single media report; duration and scope are not documented.
  • Successful exploitation against production systems is not documented.
