EV-0302Offensive cyber capabilities & misuseControlled safety experimentReal-world effectEvidence quality: Moderate

GPT-6 Cyber: strong exploit capabilities, 81 high-severity Firefox flaws reported

According to the system card, GPT-6 Cyber is a variant of GPT-6 Astra specialized for security work, which OpenAI rates as “Critical” in cybersecurity. According to OpenAI, the model produced evidence for 81 novel Firefox vulnerabilities that Mozilla classified as high severity; according to Irregular, it completed all 11 scenarios of a benchmark at least once.

Observed behaviour
According to the system card, the model was trained with reinforcement learning on tasks including developing exploits and searching codebases for vulnerabilities; in ExploitGym's honeypot test it exploited the honeypot in 0.07% of rollouts without deployment safety instructions (GPT-5.6 Sol: 49.5%) and in 0% with them. According to Irregular, it completed all 11 CyScenarioBench scenarios at least once (GPT-6 Astra: 8) and solved 90 of 288 FrontierCyber challenges (GPT-6 Astra: 96), with no successful attacks on fully hardened targets. According to OpenAI, it produced evidence and reproducible tests for 81 novel Firefox vulnerabilities that Mozilla classified as high severity.
Demonstrated consequences
Mozilla's security advisory for Firefox 159 of 27 October 2026 lists several fixed vulnerabilities classified as high impact with OpenAI as the reporter. Harm to third parties is not documented.
Severity
None
Attribution
The capabilities and the Firefox findings are attributed to the model according to OpenAI (self-report). Mozilla's advisory names OpenAI staff and “OpenAI Codex Security” as reporters, not explicitly GPT-6 Cyber. According to Irregular, the benchmark results come from an evaluation carried out together with OpenAI. (Attribution confidence: High)
Independent confirmation
Partial
Rationale of the assessment
The system card and the announcement are self-reports by the vendor. Irregular, an external party, reports on its own evaluation but worked with OpenAI. Mozilla's advisory independently confirms that OpenAI is listed as reporter of high-impact Firefox vulnerabilities, but not the number or the model's role.

Alternative explanations

  • Some of the Firefox reports may stem from humans or other tools (such as “Codex Security”); Mozilla's advisory does not name the model.

Methodological limitations

  • The system card and announcement are OpenAI self-reports; the figure of 81 vulnerabilities appears only there.
  • Irregular evaluated together with OpenAI; benchmark scores are not measurements of real attacks.
  • The model is designed for authorized security work; according to the sources the findings served defense. Misuse is not documented.
