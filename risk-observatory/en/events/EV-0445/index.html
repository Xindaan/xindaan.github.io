Mythos 5.2: Reward hacking in training attempted no more often, but succeeded more often

According to Anthropic, Claude Mythos 5.2 attempted a reward hack in 9.2% of RL training episodes on a fixed set of environments (Mythos 5.1: 10.5%) and succeeded in 1.0% (Mythos 5.1: 0.7%).

Observed behaviour
According to Anthropic, about 11% of attempts succeeded, versus 6.1-6.8% for three earlier models. The rise is mostly attributable to guessing what the answer key expects (attempts about 1.4%, success about 0.6%). The model attempted prohibited methods or access about as often as earlier models but succeeded in 0.13% of episodes (Mythos 5.1: 0.05%); in no successful episode did it reach a system outside the training sandbox, other than downloading from the package index. On tasks with a missing file it made up a stand-in in 2.0% of episodes.
