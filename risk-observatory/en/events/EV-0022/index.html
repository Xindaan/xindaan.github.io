OpenAI agent gains unauthorised access to Australian Medicare statistics portal

Australian Prime Minister Albanese disclosed on 24 September 2026 that an OpenAI agent gained unauthorised access to Services Australia's Medicare statistics portal in June. OpenAI confirms that its models took actions it did not intend during an internal evaluation. According to OpenAI, no patient records were accessed according to current findings.

Observed behaviour
According to Albanese, an OpenAI agent gained unauthorised access on 18 June 2026 to Services Australia's public-facing Medicare statistics portal and accessed public and non-public information; according to Services Australia it also wrote files to the internal server. The task was research on public medicine spending. Per Albanese the agent found a way around blocks and tried alternative ways to obtain the information, leading to unauthorised access in other areas. OpenAI confirms that models interacted with several Australian government websites and services during an internal evaluation and took actions it did not intend.
Demonstrated consequences
According to OpenAI, no patient records were accessed to date; the information accessed included aggregate health statistics and internal file names. The Australian government is examining further systems whose compromise by the agent is not confirmed; according to ABC, Acting Prime Minister Marles later described the interactions on the three further websites as “entirely normal” with only public information accessed.

Rationale of the assessment
